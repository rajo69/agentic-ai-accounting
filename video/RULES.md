# Rules for the launch video (read before writing any composition)

Derived from `.claude/plan/DESIGN.md` (sections 2, 3, 4, 6, 9) and the reference digest in
`.claude/plan/stages/S7-video-a.md`. The reference's lesson: the prompt is 10% of the result, these rules are the
other 90%.

## 1. Render contract
- Remotion, TypeScript, 1920x1080, 30 fps, 100 to 120 s. Everything is a pure function of `useCurrentFrame()`.
  No wall-clock, no `Date.now()`, no `setTimeout`, no CSS transitions or keyframes (they do not render frame by
  frame). Use `interpolate` and `spring` from `remotion` only.
- Fonts: Geist and Geist Mono from `public/fonts` via `staticFile`, Instrument Serif via `@remotion/google-fonts`.
  Wait for fonts with `delayRender` so the first frame is right.
- Product footage: either (a) clips recorded from the demo at `?skip_onboarding=1` in `public/clips/` (gitignored,
  regeneration steps in `public/clips/README.md`) played with `<OffthreadVideo>`, or (b) the landing's scene
  components ported into `src/scenes/` and driven by frame. Prefer (b) for the categorise and explain beats, (a) for
  reconciliation, documents and team.
- Audio: the voice-over is one file per beat in `public/vo/` (gitignored), generated by `scripts/tts.py` from
  `script.md`; scene timings are set from the measured durations of those files, never guessed.
- Captions burned in, Geist 32 px, bottom third, one line at a time, no background box; a hairline rule above them.
- Render with `npx remotion render Launch out/launch.mp4`; `out/` is gitignored.

## 2. Look (bans, from DESIGN.md section 2)
No gradient text except the one hero word "evidence" in the aurora stops; no glassmorphism; no three equal cards
with coloured icons; no stats with invented numbers; no drifting blob orbs; no purple glow behind headlines; no
centred-everything; no emoji; no superlatives; no uniform 16 px radius; no coloured drop-shadow glows; no spring
bounce on layout; no fade-up on every element; no placeholder avatars; no stock "AI" imagery; no cursor that moves
in straight lines at constant speed (ease it, out-quart, with a 150 ms press scale 0.98).

What it is instead: dark canvas `#09090c`, hairlines `#26262e`, Geist, mono numerals, the product shown large with a
12 px frame and a 1 px top highlight, one container that never cuts within a beat (states morph in place; content
swaps behind a 120 ms opacity dip, not a blur), left-aligned serif headlines in sentence case ending with a full
stop, the aurora only behind the title and the close.

## 3. Motion
Reveals: opacity 0 to 1, y 12 to 0, 600 ms, out-expo, stagger 50 ms, only on headlines and frames. UI state 200 ms
out-quart. The one signature animation is the categorise beat: rows receive confidence that counts up in mono, tier
chips land, the drawer slides in from the right (480 px) with reasoning and three risk rules ticking. Everything else
is quiet. Hold every reading beat long enough to read it aloud once at a normal pace.

## 4. Copy
British English, sentence case, no exclamation marks. Only numbers verifiable in the repository (34 endpoints, 93
tests, eight risk rules, one audit row per decision); never accuracy or time saved. The demo is called "demo" and
labelled synthetic on screen where it appears. Model names may appear once (Haiku 4.5 first, Sonnet 4.6 when unsure).
The honesty line is in the script and stays in: the demo is a synthetic company; the live pipeline runs on a connected
Xero organisation.

## 5. Sound
Voice: the owner's cloned voice via Fish Audio (`scripts/tts.py`, free S2.1 Pro backend), normalised to -16 LUFS in
S8. Music: one royalty-free track (YouTube Audio Library), -18 dB under the voice, fade out on the close card.
UI sounds optional and only if they sit exactly on the frame of the change they mark.

## 6. Feedback loop
After each rough cut: score the render on clarity, pacing, legibility, brand fidelity and honesty (1 to 10), name
the lowest, fix that one thing, re-render. Three rounds at most per cut, and show the owner between rounds with the
`out/launch.mp4` path and the scores.
